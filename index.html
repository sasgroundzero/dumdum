Phone

Make this phone dumb

About 10 minutes. Nothing gets deleted. Every app stays installed, it just stops opening.

Put DumDum on your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open DumDum from the new icon from now on.

Turn the screen black and white

Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters

Turn on Color Filters and pick Grayscale.

Make it boring

  1. Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion: on.
  2. Settings → Wallpaper: choose a plain black colour.
  3. Settings → Notifications: turn off Allow Notifications for every app except Phone, Messages and Mail.

Pick your one directions app

The Directions button opens this one. Allow only this one in the next steps.

Block everything, all day

Settings → Screen Time → Downtime

  1. If Screen Time is off, turn on App & Website Activity first.
  2. Turn on Scheduled, Every Day.
  3. Set From 4:00 AM To 3:59 AM. That covers the whole day.

Allow only the five

Settings → Screen Time → Always Allowed

  1. Tap the minus next to everything you can remove (FaceTime, and Maps if you chose Google Maps).
  2. Tap the plus next to Messages, Mail, Safari and your directions app.
  3. Phone is always allowed and cannot be removed.

Safari is your internet search. If DumDum itself shows a time-limit screen later, use the Phone, Messages, Mail, Maps and Safari icons directly. They do the same job.

Stop new apps, keep the old ones

Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases

  1. Turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  2. Installing Apps: Don't Allow.
  3. Deleting Apps: Don't Allow.

Lock it so you can't cheat

Settings → Screen Time → Lock Screen Time Settings

  1. Have someone you trust type a passcode you don't know. Or write one down, set it, and leave the paper somewhere inconvenient.
  2. Go back to Downtime and turn on Block at Downtime.

Without this step, every blocked app offers an "Ignore Limit" button.

Clear the Home Screen

  1. Drag Phone, Messages, Mail, your directions app and Safari (or DumDum) onto one page.
  2. Press and hold empty space, tap the dots above the dock, untick every other page, tap Done.

The hidden apps are still in the App Library, greyed out and blocked.

To undo: Settings → Screen Time → Downtime → turn off Scheduled (needs the passcode), then turn off Color Filters. Your apps come straight back.
